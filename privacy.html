Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

ClipFree AI is a browser-based creator tool. Settings you enter into this static build, including an OAuth Client ID and optional Gemini API key, are stored in your browser's local storage on the device you use. This build does not include a ClipFree server database.

When you connect Google/YouTube, Google handles authentication. The app requests YouTube creator permissions needed to upload and manage videos and read-only YouTube Analytics access used by the visible analytics dashboard. Access tokens are kept in browser memory for the active session and are not intentionally sent to a ClipFree-owned backend.

When you use Gemini, your prompt/script request and the API key are sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API. Google controls that service and its data practices. Do not enter secrets other than keys specifically intended for client use, and apply appropriate API-key restrictions in Google Cloud/AI Studio where available.

ClipFree does not sell Google user data, use connected YouTube data for advertising, or use connected Google user data to train an AI model. Generated media remains on your device until you choose to upload it or download it.

You can clear locally saved settings through your browser's site-data controls. You can revoke Google account access from your Google account's connected-app settings.
